Scope
-----

This increment adds the smallest native WebKit host needed by the fixed
composition stack:

* `WKWebViewSurface` keeps WebKit types behind a PImpl and requires AppKit main
  thread construction, mutation, inspection, close, and destruction.
* A retained WKWebView can attach, detach, reattach, and close idempotently.
* Bounds are AppKit points in the top-left document coordinate convention;
  `CanvasEmbeddedContainerView` is flipped so WebKit frames align with Metal
  ink/chrome coordinates.
* Visibility, invalid geometry suppression, and the native hit-test gate are
  covered. The middle composition container remains the owner of the broader
  embedded interaction policy.
* Navigation delegate/Ready semantics, input adapters, IME, Electron control,
  and real content synchronization are intentionally out of scope and remain
  pending.
